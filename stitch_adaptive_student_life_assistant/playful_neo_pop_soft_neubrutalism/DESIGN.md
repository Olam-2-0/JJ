## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-spirited, welcoming, and gamified student experience. Blending the graphic punch of Neo-Pop with the tactile clarity of Soft Neubrutalism, the aesthetic removes academic anxiety and replaces it with momentum, visual delight, and effortless readability.

The target audience spans high school and university students seeking focused work sessions, habit building, and adaptive learning without cognitive overload. The emotional tone is energetic yet calming, optimistic, approachable, and distinctly non-corporate.

Visual hallmarks include:
- Ultra-generous layout whitespace that eliminates clutter.
- High-impact condensed display typography paired with humanist, readable body text.
- Characteristic cloud speech bubbles, illustrative starbursts, and friendly character integration.
- Crisp flat surfaces with rounded-3xl containment and zero heavy drop shadows or glassy blurs.

## Colors

The palette relies on a warm, earthy canvas base contrasted against high-saturation coral, grounding charcoal, and gentle pastel accents. The interface is strictly daylight-driven; dark mode surfaces are intentionally excluded to maintain an upbeat, journal-like physical atmosphere.

- **Primary Canvas Background (`#EFECE6` / `#F5F3EF`):** A soft, warm paper cream that reduces glare while maintaining warmth.
- **Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp pure white providing pristine contrast against the cream canvas. Outlined with subtle hairline boundaries (`#E2E0D8`).
- **Primary Coral Red (`#FF4D52`):** Delivers intense focal energy, reserved for oversized headline treatments, motivational hero phrases, and high-priority action targets.
- **Off-Black / Charcoal (`#18181B`):** Applied to primary command buttons, key navigational text, and strong micro-copy for absolute legibility.
- **Soft Accent Containers:**
  - Soft Pastel Pink (`#FFE4E4`): Playful supportive blocks, alerts, and badge backdrops.
  - Sky Blue (`#E3F0FF` / `#4D9FFF`): Focus timers, cognitive tasks, and secondary CTAs.
  - Warm Butter Yellow (`#FEF3C7`): Streaks, rewards, tips, and achievements.
- **Negative Rules:** Absolutely no blurred glassmorphic surfaces, neon glow drop-shadows, or moody black background layers.

## Typography

The type system creates an immediate poster-like aesthetic. It pairs **Anton**, an imposing condensed geometric display face rendered in all-caps, with **Questrial**, an open and highly legible grotesque for interface navigation and continuous study materials, and **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next** for clear, accessible labels.

- **Display & Section Headers:** Set in uppercase Anton in Primary Coral Red (`#FF4D52`) or stark Charcoal (`#18181B`). Tight tracking and tight vertical leading replicate physical screen-printed graphics.
- **Narrative & System Copy:** Set in Questrial with generous line spacing to ensure prolonged screen comfort during intensive focus blocks.
- **Interactive Badges & Pill Labels:** Rendered in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next in semi-bold and bold weights with subtle positive letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy celebrates hyper-clarity and visual breathing room. Rather than filling every pixel, the system uses intentional negative space on the warm cream canvas to frame cards, mascots, and callout bubbles.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** A 12-column layout framed by generous `margin` (3rem to 5rem) and standard `gutter` (1.5rem). Components float as distinct modules rather than tightly tiled widgets.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 2rem margins, transitioning multi-column card arrays into pairs.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 1.25rem canvas margin. Stack items vertically, allowing full-width pill buttons and touch targets with a minimum height of 48px.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Section headers are isolated by `space-2xl` or `space-3xl` gaps, preventing visual competition between distinct study tools.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation is attained through flat planar contrast, clean boundaries, and volumetric shape language rather than layered shadows.

- **Flat Layering:** Surfaces exist on strict flat tiers: Canvas Base (`#EFECE6`) -> Surface Card (`#FFFFFF` or soft pastel tint) -> Interactive Pill Element (`#18181B` or `#FF4D52`).
- **Crisp Outlines:** White and light-tinted cards employ a hairline stroke (`1px` or `1.5px` solid `#E2E0D8`) to preserve edge definition against the cream backdrop.
- **Shadow Suppression:** Standard ambient and drop shadows are completely disabled (`box-shadow: none`). When an active press state requires tactile confirmation, elements translate 2px downward rather than fading shadows.
- **Cloud Callouts:** Cloud shapes and conversation balloons utilize pure `#FFFFFF` fill with rounded bubble silhouettes, overlapping adjacent cards to establish effortless natural depth.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is defined by ultra-soft contours and stadium pill silhouettes, communicating friendliness, warmth, and playfulness.

- **Cards & Modules:** Utilize generous `rounded-3xl` (1.5rem to 2rem / 24px-32px), creating smooth, pebble-like surfaces.
- **Buttons, Tags, & Search Bars:** Strictly full pill profiles (`rounded-full` / 9999px border-radius) for an inviting, tactile touch affordance.
- **Speech Bubbles & Clouds:** Distinctive organic cloud scallops with circular trailing anchor pointers to frame system prompts, mascot suggestions, and daily quotes.
- **Icon Enclosures:** Enclosed within squircle or full-circle soft pastel discs (`#FFE4E4`, `#E3F0FF`, `#FEF3C7`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Buttons:** Bold pill containers (`rounded-full`) filled in Stark Charcoal (`#18181B`) with crisp White text, or Coral Red (`#FF4D52`) with White text. Sized at `48px` to `56px` height with generous horizontal padding (`2rem`). Hover triggers subtle upward scale (1.02x) without shadow distortion.
- **Secondary / Action Pills:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1.5px` border in `#E2E0D8` and charcoal typography.
- **Tertiary Buttons:** Minimalist text buttons in Charcoal or Coral, underlined solely on active hover.

### Cloud Speech Bubble Callouts
- Pure White floating organic speech containers with distinct rounded lobe paths.
- Holds motivational prompts, streak reminders, and mascot dialogue.
- Placed dynamically overlapping character artwork or section margins.

### Cards & Modules
- Solid pure white base (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px border (`#E2E0D8`) and `rounded-3xl` corners.
- Internal content padded generously with `space-lg` to `space-xl`.
- Headers within cards default to Anton (`headline-md`) or bold Questrial (`headline-sm`).
- Feature illustration or icon placed centered at the top within a pastel squircle container.

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped tags with `0.5rem` vertical and `1rem` horizontal padding.
- Tinted fills using pastel red (`#FFE4E4`), sky blue (`#E3F0FF`), or butter yellow (`#FEF3C7`) with high-contrast saturated text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Pill inputs (`rounded-full`) or soft-cornered modules (`rounded-2xl`) in `#FFFFFF` with `1.5px` borders in `#E2E0D8`.
- Focus state switches border color to `#18181B` with zero glow rings.
- Large typographic input values (`body-lg`) for immediate clarity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Custom rounded-xl square checkboxes (24px) and circular radios (24px).
- Inactive state: Pure white with `#E2E0D8` stroke.
- Checked state: Charcoal `#18181B` or Coral Red (`#FF4D52`) background with a crisp white icon indicator.

### Gamified Progress & Streak Counters
- Progress bars designed as chunky horizontal pills (12px to 16px track height) with a cream/light grey trough and solid Coral Red or Sky Blue filled indicator.
